Dibacheh is an offline study planner. Split each lesson into sessions, get reviews scheduled with the Leitner method, and the whole plan rearranges itself after every change. Your data stays on your computer.

## Features

- Plan by day, week, month or the whole way to your last review, vertical or horizontal
- "+" menu: lesson, routine, one-off task, project, your own types and ready-made templates
- Leitner reviews with adjustable intervals, or none at all for a lesson
- Reports with weekly and monthly scores
- HTML export to read offline on your phone
- Daily backup, also copied to your OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud folder
- 13 languages, Solar Hijri and Gregorian calendars, light and dark mode, sizes from 50% to 200%

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/sdmjrfp/Dibacheh/releases/latest)**. You do not need to install Python or anything else.

The app has no paid signature, so your system warns you the first time. On Mac: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway. On Windows: More info, Run anyway.

The only time the app itself goes online is a background check for a new version, which you can turn off in Settings. It sends none of your data.
